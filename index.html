About

Research Scientist · International Institute of Information Technology Hyderabad (IIIT‑H)

I am a Research Scientist at IIIT Hyderabad. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Kanpur in December 2025, advised by Prof. Sandeep K. Shukla.

My research focuses on the early detection and containment of ransomware on endpoint systems. I have worked on detection methods that use hardware performance counters, API call sequences, registry activity, and file‑trap triggers to identify malicious behaviour before significant damage occurs.

My broader research interests include malware analysis, intrusion detection, deception based defences against advanced persistent threats, the misuse of living off the land binaries (LOLBins), detection of algorithmically generated domains, and the application of these techniques to IoT and edge environments.

Research Interests

  • Systems Security — malware analysis & detection, ransomware, advanced persistent threats (APT), threat modeling, YARA rules
  • Network Security
  • IoT Security
  • Applied machine learning for security
